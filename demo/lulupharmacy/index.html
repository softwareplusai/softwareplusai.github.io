About

A counter inside the mall.

LuLu Pharmacy is on the ground floor of Oman Avenues Mall in Al Khuwair, Muscat, at unit HMS 01. The mall directory describes it as a place for the products and services that help you stay healthy.

People writing on Google call the shop clean and organized, and easy to use for a quick or urgent stop. The same reviews say prices can sit a little higher than at other pharmacies, and that service is not always consistent. The public rating is 3.4 from 28 reviews.

The pictures on this page were made for it. They are not photographs of the shop.
