01 — Services

What we can build for you

Two paths. Choose the one that fits.

For founders & small businesses

A product you can put in front of customers

You have an idea, or a business that still depends on too much manual work. We turn that into software people can use.

  • MVP & product build A web or mobile app with AI built in, ready for customers in a few weeks.
  • AI assistant for your business A chatbot on your website, WhatsApp, or Telegram that answers customers and books appointments.
  • Automate the repetitive work Invoices, emails, spreadsheets, and CRM updates handled by software, so those tasks take less of your team's time.

For enterprises & government

AI your security team can approve

You need AI that passes a security review, connects to the systems you already use, and keeps working as more people rely on it.

  • Private AI infrastructure Open models on your own GPUs or private cloud, with access control and monitoring.
  • Knowledge assistants & search Assistants that answer from your documents, wikis, and databases, and show where each answer came from.
  • Systems integration AI added to the ERP, CRM, and older systems you already run.
  • Custom software & DevOps Platforms, data pipelines, and deployment tools your team can keep running.

03 — Process

How a project runs

Four steps, from a free call to a system your team can run.

  1. Week 0 · Free call

    Understand

    A 30-minute call about your goal, who will use the system, what data you have, and the limits we have to respect. You leave knowing what we can build.

  2. Week 1–2 · Proposal

    Design

    A written scope, a technical outline, a timeline, and a fixed price, so you know what you are buying.

  3. Weekly demos

    Build

    The work is split into short milestones, with a working demo every week. You see the progress and tell us what to change.

  4. Launch & after

    Operate

    After launch you get 30 days of support. Then your team can run it, or you can keep us on a monthly retainer.

05 — FAQ

Questions we hear often

Do you work with individuals and small businesses, or only enterprises?

Both. Many projects start with a founder or a small team and a clear idea. The scope and the price match the stage you are at.

How much does a project cost?

The price follows the scope, and we agree it before any work starts. The first call is free. Within a week you receive a written proposal with a clear budget.

How long does it take?

An AI Readiness Sprint takes two weeks. Most products and assistants go live in four to eight weeks. Larger systems are split into milestones.

Who owns the code and data?

You do. At the end of the project you receive the source code, the setup, the documentation, and your data.

Can my data stay private?

Yes. We can run the models on your own servers or in a private cloud, so sensitive data stays on your network. We can agree to keep the details private before you share them.

Do you work remotely, or only in Oman?

We are based in Muscat. We work with clients in Oman, across the GCC, and remotely anywhere else.

What happens after launch?

Every build includes support after launch. After that period you can run it yourself, or keep us on a monthly retainer for monitoring and improvements.
